About

Nic was raised in Galesburg, Illinois, a small, historic Midwestern town that produced more than its share of inventors, poets, and entrepreneurs, among them Carl Sandburg, George Washington Gale Ferris Jr., and Whitcomb L. Judson. Growing up there gave Nic an admiration for artistry and an ambition to make things of his own.

In 2011 Nic moved to Chicago to attend Loyola University Chicago, graduating cum laude in 2015 with a B.B.A. in Marketing. The City of Broad Shoulders left a lasting mark, and he still makes it back every year.

After college, Nic moved to Memphis, Tennessee, to start his career. Memphis had been a second home since childhood family visits, and he maintains it has the best chicken wings anywhere.

In early 2021, in the middle of the pandemic and without knowing a soul in town, Nic moved to Austin, Texas. He still lives there with his best friend, Thurman, a dog whose DNA test turned up 14 distinct breeds.

When he isn't chasing squirrels at the park with Thurman, Nic works in mixed media (watercolor, acrylic, charcoal, and pencil) and shoots medium-format 120 film. You might also find him noodling on guitar, powerlifting, writing, baking bread and pastries from scratch, or imagining live concert productions while he listens to music.

That last habit led Nic to found gel, a sensory innovation workshop that designs accessible platforms and experiences to transform sights, sounds, and feelings.
