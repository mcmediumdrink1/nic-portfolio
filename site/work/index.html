Work

Data analytics often feels esoteric to the very people who stand to gain the most from it. Measuring and interpreting data is hard, and turning complex calculations into something a non-specialist can act on is harder.

Then comes a second problem: in the push for clean, easy-to-digest analysis, imagination is usually the first thing to go.

There is another way: treat measurement, analysis, and visualization as much an art as a science. Nic works with technology and data not as a means to an end, but as a way of telling stories.

Background

Nic has spent over a decade in marketing and advertising at VMLY&R and GSD&M, where he is a senior analytics manager. Much of that time went to supporting the U.S. Department of Defense recruiting mission for the U.S. Navy (2016–2020) and the U.S. Air Force, Air Force Reserve, Air National Guard, and U.S. Space Force (2020 onward).

Today he leads paid media measurement for White Castle across Retail and Restaurant, working with partners on incrementality and brand lift studies to show what media actually changes: memory, consideration, and intent to buy. For the Air Force, he warehouses marketing data from every channel in Google BigQuery so teams work from a single source, and builds automation that retires manual reporting work.

Across both, the goal is the same: measure the full funnel, from first impression to lead or purchase, and turn the results into clear decisions about where to invest next.

Experience includes

  • Incrementality and brand lift measurement with exposed vs. matched control groups
  • Attribution, forecasting, and audience segmentation to guide media investment
  • Cross-channel data warehousing in Google BigQuery, with SQL and Python pipelines
  • Full website analytics and media tracking implementation through tag and pixel setup
  • Event-driven data layers that scale across Google Analytics 4 and Adobe Analytics
  • A/B and multivariate test design, focused on personalization, user journeys, and lead generation
  • Integrated dashboards in Tableau and Looker Studio
  • Python automation for data collection, ETL, and analysis notebooks
  • Web design and development, including this site

Beyond the dashboard: Nic takes the same idea, data as a medium for creation and not just interpretation, further as founder of gel, a sensory innovation workshop crafting spectacle for the mind's eye.
